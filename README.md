# Sales-Performance-Dashboard

**1. Project Title**

Sales Performance Dashboard – Revenue and Profitability Analysis

**2. Short Description**

The Sales Performance Dashboard analyzes business sales data to evaluate revenue, profitability, order volume, average order value, and profit margins.

It provides an overview of sales performance over time and across product categories, individual products, and store-related dimensions. The dashboard helps users understand sales trends, compare performance, and identify areas that may require business attention.

**3. Tech Stack**

Power BI: Data visualization, KPI reporting, and interactive dashboard development.

**4. Data Source**

The raw dataset used for this project was acquired from Maven Analytics. The data was used to perform analysis, identify trends, evaluate key performance indicators (KPIs), and develop interactive visualizations in Power BI.

**5. Features / Highlights**

Total Revenue analysis.

Total Profit monitoring.

Total Orders tracking.

Average Order Value (AOV) analysis.

Profit Margin monitoring.

Monthly sales and performance trends.

Sales performance by product category.

Product-level performance analysis.

Store-related performance analysis.

Interactive visualizations for exploring sales and profitability.

KPI-focused reporting for business performance monitoring.

**6. Business Problem / Solutions**

**Business Problem:**

Businesses need to monitor sales performance and profitability to understand whether they are meeting their objectives. Without consolidated reporting, comparing revenue, profit, order volume, and product performance can be time-consuming and may limit the identification of business trends.

**Solution:**

The Sales Performance Dashboard brings important sales KPIs together in an interactive Power BI report. It enables users to monitor revenue and profit, evaluate average order value and profit margins, track monthly performance, and compare results across product categories, products, and stores.

**Business Value:**

Improves visibility into sales performance.

Supports revenue and profitability monitoring.

Helps identify high-performing and underperforming products or categories.

Enables comparisons across time periods and business dimensions.

Supports data-driven sales and business planning.
